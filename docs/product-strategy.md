# Orvessian product and business strategy — 27 September 2026

## Proposition

An ongoing governance workspace for AI agents, model deployments, decisions, outcomes and control observations, backed by independently checkable receipts. Build the useful customer workflow first; verification provides the evidence foundation. [Current capabilities and limits](platform-architecture.md) are the product authority.

## Customer and value

Start with a design partner operating a consequential AI workflow and a named engineering owner and reviewer. Help them understand reporting activity, compare compatible adjudicated outcomes, investigate exceptions and retrieve a portable evidence package. Measure integration effort, investigation time, evidence completeness and repeat usage. Do not equate record volume with commercial demand.

Legal case types, manufacturing lines and vehicles are domain-specific expansion views, not shipped vertical products. Use opaque identifiers, explicit dimensions and versioned evaluators. Accuracy requires labels and clear denominators; descriptive cohort changes are not causal evidence about switching models.

## Product boundaries

Our intended differentiator is governance reporting connected to independently
verifiable, blockchain-anchored evidence. Customers should be able to move from
an aggregate metric to a decision, then export and check the supporting record.
Cryptographic commitments make alterations detectable against a checked anchor;
they do not make the entire database/application immutable or prove capture
completeness or AI correctness. The [roadmap](roadmap-and-decisions.md) now gives
agent visibility, decision metrics, alerts, repeatable reports and integration
explicit acceptance criteria alongside that evidence experience.

- Accept bounded structured UTF-8 JSON. Never host source conversations, documents, media or raw sensor streams on the customer's behalf.
- Persist private structured records, signatures and proof openings under access controls; they can be sensitive even without source content.
- Provide operational reporting and evidence investigation. Automatic rules/alerts and incident lifecycle are next development work; real-time enforcement stays with customer controls.
- Use Base anchoring with a gas-paying service wallet. Customers do not need crypto assets. No new L1 or token launch is required.
- Keep commitment integrity, recording-service signature, source authenticity, outcome quality and chain confirmation separate.

## Competitive direction

Maintain the [competitor register](competitor-research.md) weekly and use the
[competitive feature backlog](roadmap-and-decisions.md#competitive-feature-backlog)
for delivery decisions. Seek broad coverage of the features our target customer
needs: inventory/ownership, activity and cost context, outcome evaluation,
investigation, rules, incident follow-up, reporting, integrations and evidence.
Use common interfaces and complementary integrations to increase coverage without
building a complete agent runtime, prompt workbench or inference gateway.

LangSmith, Langfuse, Braintrust and Arize are both adjacent competitors and
potential sources of structured observations. Fiddler, Credo AI and Holistic AI
set expectations for controls and organisational governance. Differentiate with
useful cross-tool review and independently checkable exports; neither privacy nor
governance is a unique claim by itself. Source truth and completeness remain
outside what anchoring proves.

Prioritise supported TypeScript/Python clients, OpenTelemetry mappings and one
LangChain/LangGraph integration, followed by provider, evaluation and workflow
connectors in the [SDK plan](sdk-integration-plan.md). These are planned additions.
Validate integration time, weekly reviewer use, exception-resolution time and
independent evidence verification before claiming competitive advantage.

## Delivery and commercial sequence

Finish the current VPS reliability run; add portal-wide verification summaries and capacity controls; build configurable governance workflows; validate a partner use case. Establish identity, environments, storage, managed keys, backup/restore and retention controls before real customer data. Agree commercial terms only after cost and operational evidence.

Monthly/annual workspace subscriptions with included accepted-event usage and measured overages are the current hypothesis. Evaluate/Operate/Enterprise remain proposed packaging, not active paid offers. Indicative website prices and allowances are recorded in the [commercial model](commercial-model.md); they require validation before charging. SLAs and support scope remain open.

Usage intelligence may later create value from permissioned aggregates. A data marketplace requires explicit rights, revocation/retention policy, privacy analysis and buyer validation. ZK can support narrow statements; it does not itself anonymize data or create permission to sell it. Native-token demand, mining allocation and exchange listing are historical secondary research, outside this launch.
