# Orvessian product and business strategy — 27 September 2026

> Historical archive — 27 September 2026. Retained for its original scope, not current launch guidance. See the [active documentation](C:/AIChain/docs/README.md).

## Proposition

An ongoing governance workspace for AI agents, model deployments, decisions, outcomes and control observations, backed by independently checkable receipts. Build the useful customer workflow first; verification provides the evidence foundation. [Current capabilities and limits](C:/AIChain/docs/platform-architecture.md) are the product authority.

## Customer and value

Start with a design partner operating a consequential AI workflow and a named engineering owner and reviewer. Help them understand reporting activity, compare compatible adjudicated outcomes, investigate exceptions and retrieve a portable evidence package. Measure integration effort, investigation time, evidence completeness and repeat usage. Do not equate record volume with commercial demand.

Legal case types, manufacturing lines and vehicles are domain-specific expansion views, not shipped vertical products. Use opaque identifiers, explicit dimensions and versioned evaluators. Accuracy requires labels and clear denominators; descriptive cohort changes are not causal evidence about switching models.

## Product boundaries

- Accept bounded structured UTF-8 JSON. Never host source conversations, documents, media or raw sensor streams on the customer's behalf.
- Persist private structured records, signatures and proof openings under access controls; they can be sensitive even without source content.
- Provide operational reporting and evidence investigation. Automatic rules/alerts and incident lifecycle are next development work; real-time enforcement stays with customer controls.
- Use Base anchoring with a gas-paying service wallet. Customers do not need crypto assets. No new L1 or token launch is required.
- Keep commitment integrity, recording-service signature, source authenticity, outcome quality and chain confirmation separate.

## Delivery and commercial sequence

Finish the current VPS reliability run; add portal-wide verification summaries and capacity controls; build configurable governance workflows; validate a partner use case. Establish identity, environments, storage, managed keys, backup/restore and retention controls before real customer data. Agree commercial terms only after cost and operational evidence.

Monthly/annual workspace subscriptions with included accepted-event usage and measured overages are the current hypothesis. Evaluate/Operate/Enterprise are proposed packaging, not active paid offers. Price, allowances, SLAs and support scope remain open.

Usage intelligence may later create value from permissioned aggregates. A data marketplace requires explicit rights, revocation/retention policy, privacy analysis and buyer validation. ZK can support narrow statements; it does not itself anonymize data or create permission to sell it. Native-token demand, mining allocation and exchange listing are historical secondary research, outside this launch.
